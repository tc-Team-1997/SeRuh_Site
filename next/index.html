Privacy & safety

What we do and don't know about you

  • You can write without an account. We never ask for your name.
  • Your browser keeps a random id so you can find and delete your own posts. It isn't linked to you, and clearing your browser data clears it.
  • No followers, no follower counts, nothing to win.
  • Every thought is screened automatically before it appears, and anyone can report one.
  • You can delete anything you wrote, at any time.

Seruh is a place to be heard — not a substitute for professional care. If you're in danger, please reach out to someone who can help right now.
